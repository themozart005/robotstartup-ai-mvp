🎯 About RoboStartup AI
RoboStartup AI is a comprehensive educational platform that teaches business fundamentals, financial literacy, and entrepreneurship through engaging, AI-powered gameplay. Students run their own robotics companies, making real business decisions while learning essential 21st-century skills.
Part of the award-winning "Makers & Moguls" educational series by LevelUp STEM Academy.
🎓 Educational Alignment

Common Core Mathematics: Ratios, statistics, mathematical modeling
NGSS Science Standards: Engineering design, systems thinking, data analysis
Social Studies: Economics, entrepreneurship, global markets
Ages: 8-14 years (adaptable difficulty levels)
Class Time: 45-90 minute flexible sessions

🌟 Key Features

Advanced AI Tutoring: Proprietary AI business logic with personalized explanations
Intelligent Multiplayer: Sophisticated multiplayer game mode with AI opponents
Multi-Phase Gameplay: Startup → R&D → Production → Sales → Investment
Real-Time Collaboration: Students compete and collaborate globally
Comprehensive Analytics: Track learning progress and outcomes
Standards-Aligned Curriculum: Complete teaching resources included


🎮 How It Works
For Students:

Build Robot Companies - Design and manufacture robots with advanced technology
Make Strategic Decisions - Manage cash flow, R&D investments, and production
Compete in Markets - Sell products and compete with AI and peer opponents
Learn from AI Tutor - Get personalized business explanations and guidance

For Educators:

Complete Lesson Plans - Ready-to-use curriculum for 6-12 sessions
Assessment Tools - Rubrics and progress tracking aligned to standards
Differentiated Instruction - Support for beginner to advanced learners
Parent Resources - Communication tools and home extension activities


🖥️ Technical Requirements
System Requirements

✅ Any device with modern web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge)
✅ Stable internet connection (school WiFi compatible)
✅ Audio capability (speakers or headphones recommended)
✅ Works on: Chromebooks, iPads, Windows, Mac, Android devices

Deployment Architecture

Frontend: React + TypeScript (Vercel hosting)
Backend: Node.js + Express + Socket.IO (Railway hosting)
AI Integration: OpenAI GPT-4 for educational content
Real-time Communication: WebSocket for multiplayer functionality
